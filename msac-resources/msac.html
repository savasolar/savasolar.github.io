More info

The Generative Delay pedal listens to the notes you play, figures out harmonies that sound good, and plays new notes that fit.

  • Chaos controls the randomness of generated harmonies
  • Bloom sets the length of each new note
  • Rate controls the speed of new notes
  • Mix controls the wet/dry volume

This pedal is an effort to push the boundaries of musical expression, and unlocks a sonic palette that was previously only achievable through hours of manual audio production.

As such, it possesses a rare combination of qualities: it is unique enough to sound unlike any other pedal, useful enough to be applicable to any style of guitar playing, and artistic enough to change the way we hear music.
